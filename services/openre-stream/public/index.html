Use this, not that

openre.stream handles the production side. openvibe.live handles the public side. You use the same account for both.

Use openre.stream for
  • Creating stream sessions and getting fresh ingest credentials.
  • Saving RTMP targets for YouTube, Twitch, Kick, or any custom endpoint.
  • Starting, pausing, and ending streams from a real operator dashboard.
  • Multistreaming to multiple platforms at once without extra software.
Use openvibe.live for
  • Your public @channel page and creator profile.
  • Browsing live channels and recent broadcasts.
  • VODs and clips that automatically tie back to your route.
  • Quick go-live if you do not need the full control room.
