Why OpenVibe

Open source
Every service runs from public repositories under permissive licenses. Self-host or join the community network.
No tracking, no ads
No third-party trackers, no behavioral advertising, no surveillance economy. Anonymous browsing where it makes sense.
Shared identity
Sign in once via auth.openvibe.network. Your themes, preferences, and modules follow you everywhere.
Modular
Each service owns its data and contracts. Capabilities and registries are first-class so federated apps can plug in.
